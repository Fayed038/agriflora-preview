01 — About

One of the Middle East's leading protein meal exporters.

Established in 2013 in the Al Hamra Industrial Zone, Ras Al Khaimah. Today Agri Flora is among the region's largest exporters of rendered and marine protein meals.

We built a supply chain to Asia, the EU and Africa on one thing: buyers knowing exactly what lands in their mill, every container, every time.

Our range covers feather meal, poultry meal, fish meal, fish oil, poultry oil and soybean meal, alongside wider agricultural commodities. Every lot ships against a written specification, with certification free from melamine, E. coli and salmonella on our tested lines.
